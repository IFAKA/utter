Why it matters

Speech is the fastest way to communicate, but you can't always say it out loud.

Meetings, public transport, hospitals, loud workplaces, or a voice you can't use. SilentBand gives people a private, hands-free channel to their devices.

🔒

Truly private

Nothing is spoken and nothing is seen. Not even the people next to you can tell you are typing.

✋

Hands & eyes free

Reply while walking, cooking or driving. The earbud reads back suggestions, so you never look at a screen.

♿

Accessible

A path to text for people with voice loss or limited hand mobility, built on muscles they still control.

How it works

Four steps, all on your own device.

01 · SENSE

16-channel biosignal band

ECG-class 24-bit analog front-ends sample the jaw, chin and throat muscles at 2,000 samples per second.

02 · DECODE

Personal syllable model

A lightweight model learns your 8 silent syllables in four short guided sessions, and ignores chewing and swallowing.

03 · PREDICT

Local language model

Next-word prediction means most words take a single syllable to confirm.

04 · TYPE

Works in any app

Text goes wherever your cursor is: messages, email, documents. Earbud audio confirms every step.

Where we are

Hardware designed. Software built. First human trials next.

Published research on silently articulated speech reaches 88-97% accuracy on small vocabularies. Our next milestone tests the lips-closed variant on our own hardware.
